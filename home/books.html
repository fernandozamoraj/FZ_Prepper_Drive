Reading tips

  • PDF opens right in the browser on any device.
  • 📖 Read opens EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2 and CBZ books right here, on any phone or computer: tap or swipe the edges (or use the arrow keys) to turn pages, ☰ for contents, A− A+ for text size, ◐ for light, sepia or dark. It remembers your page on each device. Copy-protected Kindle books can't be opened.
  • EPUB in an app instead: on an iPhone or iPad, tap it and choose Books. On Android, tap it and open it with a reader app (Google Play Books, ReadEra). On a Mac, the Books app opens it. On Windows, use Thorium Reader, SumatraPDF or Calibre from the Software page.
  • Add a document (top of this page) copies a PDF or e-book into the kit, so it goes along when the kit is copied to a thumb drive. Anyone on the Wi-Fi can add; only the computer that runs the kit can remove (removed files go to uploads/.trash). Files copied by hand into uploads/<category>/ show up too.
  • A whole folder of your own books and audiobooks (without copying it): add the folder on the Settings page.
  • Audiobooks play here and keep going to the next part; this device remembers where you stopped. Use Download on a part to keep it on a phone.
