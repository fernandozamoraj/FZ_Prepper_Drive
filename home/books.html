Reading tips

  • PDF opens right in the browser on any device.
  • EPUB: on an iPhone or iPad, tap it and choose Books. On Android, tap it and open it with a reader app (Google Play Books, ReadEra). On a Mac, the Books app opens it. On Windows, use Thorium Reader, SumatraPDF or Calibre from the Software page.
  • Your own books and audiobooks: add their folders on the Settings page.
  • Audiobooks play here and keep going to the next part; this device remembers where you stopped. Use Download on a part to keep it on a phone.
