Add more movies

← Movies · Three ways to put more films on the kit. Only add films you're allowed to download and share: public domain or an open license such as Creative Commons.

  1. From the Internet Archive (easiest)

    While you have internet, find a film on archive.org (the Feature Films, Film Noir, Silent Films and SciFi / Horror collections are good places to look; the item page shows its license). Copy the address of its page, then on the computer that runs the kit:

    The kit picks the item's MP4 that plays in every browser, downloads it, and lists it under My movies. Options: --title "Name", --year 1950, --category comedy (comedy, drama, mystery, adventure, horror, open-movies, my-movies). It warns you when archive.org shows no free license for the item.

  2. From a direct link to a video file

    For a film you can download straight from a site that offers it freely (for example Blender Studio's open movies):

  3. Copy video files into the movies folder

    Put any video file (from a USB stick, a camera, your own videos) into this folder on the kit, or into one of the category folders next to it (comedy, drama, horror…):

    movies/my-movies/

    Reload the Movies page and it's there, named after the file (e.g. Family Picnic 2019.mp4). No command needed.

Which files play in the browser

FilePlays here?
.mp4 / .m4v (H.264 video)Yes, on every phone, tablet and computer. Best choice.
.webmYes in Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Android; recent iPhones too.
.mkv, .avi, .movSometimes. If not, use Download and play it with VLC or mpv from the Software page.

Removing a film

Delete its file from the movies folder. For films added with movies add, also remove its entry from movies/my-movies.json (or leave it: it will just show as not downloaded).
